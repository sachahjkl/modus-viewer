**The bar anywhere, in the machined look.** The bar of the machine is a plate of the toolkit's machined finish, and its
handle moves it: docked to any edge (a column on a side), floating over the screen, or in a small window of its own,
for a second monitor or a screen with no overlay. Its pin keeps it shown. Its lamp is green, yellow or red with the
round trip, and Disconnect is red. The place and the pin are kept for the next run. The message while the viewer
connects, and after an end, is a plate in the middle of the window.
