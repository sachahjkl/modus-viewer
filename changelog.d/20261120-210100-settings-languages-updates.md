**Settings, French, updates and a crash report.** The viewer has the toolkit's settings page: the theme, the language,
where the messages go, and its own settings (the default scale, the shortcut of the input, the pin of the bar). It
speaks English and French. It checks its releases and updates itself, says when its last run crashed, keeps the place of
its window, and tells what it is under About. The form lists the last machines whose screen showed.
