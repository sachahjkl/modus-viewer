**The window.** The viewer shows the screen of a modus machine at its size, scaled to the window, with a mark at the
place of the pointer of the machine. It keeps its viewer key in the store of secrets of the account, and shows the line
that allows the key in a console of the machine. With the input on, it sends the keys and the pointer. When a
connection ends, the viewer says why in one sentence, with the code of the end.
