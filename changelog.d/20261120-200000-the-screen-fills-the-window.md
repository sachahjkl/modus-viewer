**The screen fills the window.** The screen of the machine takes the whole window, at 1:1 when it fits, else as large
as it fits with its proportions, in a window titled with the name of the machine or in full screen. A thin bar shows
when the pointer rests at the top edge, with the round trip, the frames of the last second, 1:1 or Fit, Full screen,
Input and Disconnect. Ctrl+Alt releases the input and gives it back, and Escape goes to the machine. Your own cursor
is the cursor, with no wait for the machine, and each motion of a frame goes as one. While the viewer connects, and
when the connection ends, it says so in the middle of the window, with Retry. `-measure` prints the round trip, the
interval between the frames and the dropped frames.
